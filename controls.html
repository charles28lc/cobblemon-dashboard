New here?

Press M to pick your starter Pokémon. Until you have one, that's what M does.

Keys

R
Throw selected Pokémon — depends on what you're doing:
  • Looking at nothing or your own Pokémon: send it out, or recall it.
  • Looking at a wild Pokémon: start a battle.
  • Looking at another player: open the player menu.
  • In a battle: hide or show the battle screen.
  • Riding: get off.
↑ ↓
Party shift up / down — choose which party Pokémon is selected.
M
Open summary — stats and moves for your selected Pokémon.
O
Hide party overlay — toggle the party bar on your screen.
Left Alt
Riding freelook — hold while riding.

Map Xaero's Minimap & World Map

The minimap is always on screen. There's no command for it — use these keys.

N
Open world map — full-screen map of everywhere you've been.
Z
Enlarge minimap
B
New waypoint — mark where you are.
U
Waypoint list
Y
Minimap settings
Defaults for the Cobblemon Official Modpack 1.8.1. Change them in Options → Controls → Key Binds.
